College Audition Guide

The college music application process is unlike any other. This guide walks through every stage, from building your school list to what happens after you hear back.

  • Chapter 1

    Understanding the Landscape

    Conservatories, university programs, liberal arts schools with music. There are many different types of music programs. What each path looks like and what it prioritizes.

  • Chapter 2

    Building Your School List

    Reach, match, and safety, and what they actually mean for music programs. What to look for beyond rankings and reputation.

  • Chapter 3

    Repertoire and Timeline

    How to choose what to play, when to lock it in, and how to structure your preparation over the months leading up to auditions.

  • Chapter 4

    Prescreens

    What they are, which schools require them, how to record well, and how to think about them separately from the live audition.

  • Chapter 5

    Live Auditions

    Logistics, nerves, what actually happens in the room, and how to recover when something goes wrong.

  • Chapter 6

    Finding the Right Fit

    Teacher relationships, campus visits, and what questions to ask that most applicants don't think to ask.

  • Chapter 7

    After the Audition

    Decisions, waitlists, financial aid, and how to evaluate your options when you have them, or when you don't.
